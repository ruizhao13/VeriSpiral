# Theorem–Claim–Evidence Audit

## Trigger

Use before promoting claims into a decision packet, abstract, README, or paper.

## Workflow

1. Split prose into atomic claims.
2. Classify each claim as theorem, citation, mechanism, experiment, or taste.
3. Bind it to an evidence artifact and visible assumptions.
4. Assign `proved`, `cited`, `verified`, `diagnostic`, or `unsupported`.
5. Promote, qualify, repair, or remove the claim.

## Validation

- Evidence strength is never weaker than the wording it supports.
- Negative evidence and forbidden overclaims remain visible.
- Conditional and empirical-only statements are labeled in place.
